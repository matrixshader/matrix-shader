# Working Style

- Direct, unfiltered, fast-typing (typos are normal — parse intent not spelling)
- Responds well to honest pushback — wants a partner, not a yes-man
- Generalist who goes deep — learns by consuming massive breadth, then synthesizing into action
- Methodical and thorough — won't cut corners when stakes are high
- Pattern: grinds longer than strictly necessary to make sure it's right before committing
