Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

The Short Version

MatrixShader does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The desktop software runs entirely offline. The website uses privacy-focused analytics with no cookies and no personal information.

Website Analytics

We use Umami, a privacy-focused, open-source analytics tool. Umami:

  • Does not use cookies
  • Does not collect personal information
  • Does not track users across websites
  • Does not store IP addresses
  • Is fully GDPR, CCPA, and PECR compliant

We collect aggregate page view counts and referrer data to understand how people find MatrixShader. No individual user can be identified from this data.

Desktop Software

The MatrixShader desktop application:

  • Runs entirely on your local machine
  • Does not connect to the internet
  • Does not send telemetry, usage data, or crash reports
  • Does not collect any personal information
  • Stores all configuration files locally in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MatrixShader\

License Keys

Red Pill license keys are validated entirely offline using cryptographic verification. No server is contacted during activation or use. Your license key is stored locally on your machine.

Payments

Purchases are processed by LemonSqueezy or the Microsoft Store. We do not directly handle, store, or have access to your payment information (credit card numbers, billing addresses, etc.). Please refer to LemonSqueezy's privacy policy or Microsoft's privacy statement for details on how they handle payment data.

Data We Store

None. We have no database of users, no email list, no account system. If you purchase through LemonSqueezy or the Microsoft Store, those platforms maintain their own transaction records per their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

MatrixShader does not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page with the new date. Since we don't collect email addresses, this page is the sole notice mechanism.
