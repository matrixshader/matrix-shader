    - Use Shadertoy API: `void mainImage(out vec4 fragColor, in vec2 fragCoord)`, iTime, iResolution
    - Alpha = 1.0 (no transparency during white room)
    - Keep the shader self-contained (no iChannel0 texture dependency)
