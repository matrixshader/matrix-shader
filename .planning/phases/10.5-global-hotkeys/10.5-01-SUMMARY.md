## Decisions Made

1. **LibraryImport over DllImport** - Required for Native AOT compatibility. Only RegisterClassExW uses DllImport due to function pointer in WNDCLASSEXW struct.

2. **MOD_NOREPEAT flag on all bindings** - Prevents continuous firing when user holds down key. Each hotkey triggers once per press.

3. **LocalAppData/MatrixShader/ for config** - Consistent with existing identity-registry.json location. User-specific, roaming not needed.
